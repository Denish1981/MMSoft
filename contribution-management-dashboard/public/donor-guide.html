Making a Contribution

How to contribute to festival funds & earn food coupons

Making a contribution supports trust activities and festival celebrations. All registered donors are eligible to contribute online or via trust volunteers.

Steps to Submit a Contribution:

  1. Click "Make Contribution": Located prominently on your Donor Portal Header or the primary action bar.
  2. Select Festival: Choose the active festival campaign you wish to contribute towards (e.g., Ganesh Utsav, Navratri, Diwali).
  3. Choose Contribution Type:
    • Standard Contribution: Fixed preset amount tier with calculated food coupons.
    • Custom Contribution: Enter any custom donation amount.
    • Miscellaneous Contribution: For specific offerings or general donations.
  4. Verify Donor Information: Ensure your Name, Mobile Number, Tower (42-46), and Flat Number are accurate.
  5. Upload Payment Proof (Recommended): Attach a screenshot or image of your UPI/bank transaction receipt for quick verification.
  6. Submit Contribution: Click Submit. Your contribution will immediately appear in your "My Contributions" list.

Contribution Approval Workflow & Receipts

Understanding status verification and official receipt generation

Pending Stage 1

Submitted & Awaiting Audit

Your contribution request is recorded. The trust finance team or volunteers cross-verify the payment proof against trust bank accounts.

Approved / Completed Stage 2

Verified & Verified

Once confirmed, your contribution status changes to Approved. Your allocated Food Coupons become official, and a trust receipt is generated.

Receipt Stage 3

Download Official Receipt

Click the "View Receipt" button on your contribution card to open, print, or download your official digital contribution receipt.

Note on Stall & Event Registrations Privilege: An approved contribution unlocks your donor privileges (such as registering stalls or special festival activity entries). Ensure your contribution is submitted early so volunteers can approve it promptly!

Single Event Registration

Registering for individual festival competitions and activities

Festivals feature competitions such as Drawing, Rangoli, Dance, Quiz, and Sports. You can register yourself or family members for any single event directly.

Method A: Via Choice Modal

On your Donor Portal, click "Register Stall / Event" on the header. Select the "Event Registration" tab, pick your desired event, and click Register Now.

Method B: Via Upcoming Events List

Scroll down on your portal to the "Upcoming Events" section. Click the "Register" button on any active event card to open its custom registration form.

What information is required?
  • Participant Name & Contact details.
  • Tower Number (42 - 46) & Flat Number (101 - 3313).
  • Any custom fields defined by event organizers (e.g., Age category, Group size, Song choice).
  • Payment proof attachment if the specific event has an entry fee.

Multiple Event Registration

Registering for multiple events in a single streamlined submission

If you or your family members wish to participate in multiple festival competitions (e.g. Dance + Rangoli + Drawing), use the batch Multiple Event Registration workflow to save time.

Steps for Bulk / Multi-Event Registration:

  1. Open Multi-Event Form: Click the "Register Multiple Events" button in your Donor Portal header or navigate to /donor-portal/register-events.
  2. Select Festival: Choose the festival whose events you want to browse.
  3. Select Target Events: Check the boxes next to all the events you wish to register for simultaneously.
  4. Fill Participant Details per Event:

    For each selected event, fill in the participant's name, age, category, or specific requirements. You can register different family members for different events in the same session!

  5. Unified Payment & Submission:

    If any selected events require registration fees, the total combined amount is displayed. Attach a single consolidated payment screenshot and submit.

Tracking Your Registrations: Go to the "My Event Registrations" tab on your portal at any time to view all your registered events, participant details, and entry statuses.
