Making a Contribution

How to contribute to festival funds & earn food coupons

Making a contribution supports trust activities and festival celebrations. All registered donors are eligible to contribute online or via trust volunteers.

Steps to Submit a Contribution:

  1. Click "Make Contribution": Located prominently on your Donor Portal Header or the primary action bar.
  2. Select Festival: Choose the active festival campaign you wish to contribute towards (e.g., Ganesh Utsav, Navratri, Diwali).
  3. Choose Contribution Type:
    • Standard Contribution: Fixed preset amount tier with calculated food coupons.
    • Custom Contribution: Enter any custom donation amount.
    • Miscellaneous Contribution: For specific offerings or general donations.
  4. Verify Donor Information: Ensure your Name, Mobile Number, Tower (42-46), and Flat Number are accurate.
  5. Upload Payment Proof (Recommended): Attach a screenshot or image of your UPI/bank transaction receipt for quick verification.
  6. Submit Contribution: Click Submit. Your contribution will immediately appear in your "My Contributions" list.

Contribution Approval Workflow & Receipts

Understanding status verification and official receipt generation

Pending Stage 1

Submitted & Awaiting Audit

Your contribution request is recorded. The trust finance team or volunteers cross-verify the payment proof against trust bank accounts.

Approved / Completed Stage 2

Verified & Verified

Once confirmed, your contribution status changes to Approved. Your allocated Food Coupons become official, and a trust receipt is generated.

Receipt Stage 3

Download Official Receipt

Click the "View Receipt" button on your contribution card to open, print, or download your official digital contribution receipt.

Note on Event Registrations Privilege: An approved contribution unlocks your donor privileges (such as special festival activity entries). Ensure your contribution is submitted early so volunteers can approve it promptly!

Household Roster & Event Registration

Pre-configuring household members and registering them for festival events

Festivals feature competitions such as Drawing, Rangoli, Dance, Quiz, and Sports. You can easily pre-save your household members and register them for festival events in a few simple clicks.

Steps to Register via Household Roster:

  1. Open Household Roster Tab: Log in to your Donor Portal and click the "Household Roster" tab.
  2. Save Household Members: Add your family members with their Full Name, Phone Number, and Email Address. Click "Save Roster" to store them securely.
  3. Click "Register for Event": Beside any saved member in your roster, click the blue "Register for Event" button.
  4. Select Festival & Events: Choose the active festival and select the events (e.g. Dance, Quiz, Drawing) that member wants to participate in.
  5. Submit Registration: Fill in any event-specific requirements (such as age group or song selection) and submit!
What information is pre-populated?
  • Primary Contact & Resident details (Tower & Flat Number).
  • Saved participant details (Name, Contact Number, Email) automatically selected from your Household Roster.

Auto-Fill & Tracking Event Registrations

Easily auto-filling saved roster details and reviewing your registered entries

When registering for festival events, you can quickly auto-fill participant details and keep track of all registrations directly in your portal.

Auto-fill Roster Dropdown

On any event registration page, use the "Select from Household Roster" dropdown to quickly pick saved family members without typing their details again.

My Event Registrations Tab

Click the "My Event Registrations" tab on your Donor Portal dashboard to view all registered events, participant names, event dates, and entry statuses.

Need Updates or Changes? If you update your Household Roster names or details, future event registrations will automatically reflect the updated details.
