Portal Registration Guide

Step-by-step instructions for new users joining the Gold Towers Mitra Mandal Trust portal

Welcome Resident / Donor! Registering for the Gold Towers portal gives you direct access to make festival donations, view instant contribution receipts, track food coupon allocations, register for community events, and view event photo albums.

4 Simple Steps to Register

1

Switch to Registration Mode

On the login page, click the "New User? Register" button located at the top right of the card.

2

Fill in Personal & Login Details

Complete all required mandatory fields:

  • Full Name: Enter your complete name.
  • Email Address (Username): Enter a valid email address used to log into your account.
  • Password: Create a secure password for your portal login.
  • Mobile Number: Enter your contact phone number.
3

Select Tower & Flat Number

Choose your resident location from the dropdown lists:

  • Tower / Block: Select from Towers 42, 43, 44, 45, or 46.
  • Flat Number: Select your flat number from the floor-wise organized options (Floors 1 to 33, Flats 01 to 13 e.g., 101, 102, ... 3313).
4

Submit & Sign In

Click the "Register as Donor" button. Once successful, switch back to Sign In mode and log in with your new credentials!

Important Tips

Mandatory Fields All registration fields are mandatory to ensure accurate issuance of donation receipts and food coupons to your flat.
Instant Access After registering, you can immediately log in to view your donor dashboard and make online contributions.
